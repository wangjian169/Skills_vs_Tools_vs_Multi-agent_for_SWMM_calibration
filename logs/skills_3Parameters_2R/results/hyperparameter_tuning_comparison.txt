SWMM ES-ILU HYPERPARAMETER TUNING ANALYSIS
============================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
Two micro-tuning runs were performed to optimize ES-ILU hyperparameters. 
Tune1 (sigma_rel=0.02) demonstrated the best overall performance with 
significantly improved validation NSE (0.9128 vs baseline 0.8168), making 
it the recommended configuration.

PERFORMANCE COMPARISON
----------------------

| Configuration          | sigma_rel | niter | ne | Calibration NSE | Validation NSE | Δ Calibration | Δ Validation |
|------------------------|-----------|-------|----|-----------------|----------------|---------------|--------------|
| Baseline               | 0.033     | 10    | 300| 0.9353          | 0.8168         | -             | -            |
| Tune1 (sigma_rel=0.02) | 0.020     | 10    | 300| 0.9308          | 0.9128         | -0.0045       | +0.0960      |
| Tune2 (niter=15)       | 0.033     | 15    | 300| 0.9356          | 0.8169         | +0.0003       | +0.0001      |

KEY FINDINGS
------------

1. **Sigma_rel Tuning (Tune1)**: 
   - Reducing sigma_rel from 0.033 to 0.020 tightened the observation constraint
   - Result: Validation NSE improved by 11.7% (0.8168 → 0.9128)
   - Trade-off: Slight decrease in calibration NSE (-0.48%)
   - Overall: Best generalization capability

2. **Iteration Tuning (Tune2)**:
   - Increasing niter from 10 to 15 iterations
   - Result: Negligible improvement in both calibration and validation
   - Conclusion: 10 iterations is sufficient for convergence

3. **Parameter Analysis**:
   - Tune1 parameters showed better alignment with true values for slope
   - All methods consistently overestimated imperviousness parameters
   - Tune1 demonstrated better parameter estimation despite slightly lower calibration NSE

RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------

1. **Primary Recommendation**: Use sigma_rel = 0.020 for future calibrations
   - Provides best validation performance (NSE = 0.9128)
   - Excellent generalization to unseen data
   - Slight calibration trade-off is acceptable for improved robustness

2. **Secondary Finding**: niter = 10 is sufficient
   - No significant benefit from increasing to 15 iterations
   - Saves computational time without sacrificing performance

3. **Overall Assessment**: 
   - Baseline configuration already provided excellent performance (NSE > 0.8)
   - Tune1 optimization provides substantial validation improvement
   - Model demonstrates strong robustness across hyperparameter variations

CONCLUSION
----------
The hyperparameter tuning exercise successfully identified sigma_rel as the 
most influential hyperparameter for improving model generalization. The 
recommended configuration (sigma_rel=0.020, niter=10, ne=300) provides 
optimal balance between calibration accuracy and validation performance, 
making the SWMM model more reliable for practical applications.
